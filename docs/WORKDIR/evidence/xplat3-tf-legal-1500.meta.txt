=== THE CONTROL, which is the part that matters ===

A high distinct count is NOT by itself evidence that the AI did anything: an ambient mover with no
player attached will produce one. Alpine assault carries a TrainEngine, and a railroad behaviour
self-moves every frame and has its transform hashed into the object CRC. So the control is to run
the same networked match with NO AI at all and see whether the activity disappears.

  networked 2-peer, 300 frames        0 AI          5 Hard AI
  twilight flame  (capacity 8)        3 distinct    300 distinct     <- AI are the activity
  alpine assault  (capacity 2)      299 distinct    299 distinct     <- AI contributed NOTHING

On twilight flame the activity vanishes without the AI, so the AI are demonstrably simulating.
On alpine the number is identical with and without them - it was measuring a train, and every
alpine-based "the AI were active" claim in this corpus is unsupported.
