The guard fired exactly ONCE, for slot=2 - the third occupied slot on a 2-start map, i.e.
precisely the predicted surplus player. That is prediction P2 of
networked-sim-freeze-diagnosis.md satisfied directly: spawned players == min(occupied, numPlayers).

The same run reported `aiPlayers=2 computers=4 sides=6`. TWO AI reported, ONE of them owning
nothing. That is the exact lie this whole investigation was about, now printed next to the truth.

4. WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
--------------------------
